## Featured Project — PropertyFlow AI

AI-powered lead qualification assistant designed for real estate agencies.

### How it works

A conversational AI assistant that:

* Qualifies incoming leads
* Understands customer requirements
* Recommends matching properties
* Captures contact information
* Automates client onboarding
* Stores leads inside a lightweight CRM system

### Business Value

PropertyFlow AI reduces manual communication for agents and ensures that potential clients receive immediate assistance without waiting for human interaction.
